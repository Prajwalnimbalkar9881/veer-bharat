History belongs to everyone

Veer Bharat is a learning project created to make the stories of India's freedom struggle easier to explore. It includes leaders, revolutionaries, social reformers and regional heroes from different communities and parts of the subcontinent.

This is a starter collection, not a complete list of every person who contributed. Keep researching and add carefully verified biographies.

Learn responsibly

Dates and historical interpretations can vary across sources. Before publishing, verify each biography using reliable references such as the National Archives of India, Parliament/official biographies, museum collections and established history books.

Respect every contribution.
